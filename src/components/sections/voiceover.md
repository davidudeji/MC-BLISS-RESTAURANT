## Filming Tips
- Record at **1x speed**, then speed up boring transitions to **1.5x** in editing
- Use **caption overlays** for the tech stack names (React, TypeScript, etc.)
- Add a **trending audio track** underneath — keep voiceover volume at 80%, music at 20%
- Use **CapCut** or **DaVinci Resolve** for auto-captions and zoom cuts
- The first 3 seconds determine if the video gets pushed — **start with the hook, not an intro**
